# Digital Lab Map Rebuild Design

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Status:** Approved for implementation planning  
**Primary reference:** `Screenshot 2026-07-26 092955.png`

## Objective

Rebuild the laboratory map so the staff view, visitor popup, scanner navigation, and evacuation views all use one accurate coordinate system. The current room rectangles and coarse public projection are not reliable enough for navigation or safety use and must not be patched incrementally.

The rebuilt map must:

- match the latest source plan, including nested rooms, walls, doors, scanner boundaries, lifts, stairs, access points, and labels;
- route visitors to the correct fingerprint checkpoint and stop there;
- display usable primary and alternate evacuation presets from the active map station;
- remove the map-specific personnel feature;
- remove the standalone public map route and show visitor maps inside the active-visit page;
- place the staff map under a new **ความปลอดภัย** navigation group.

## Decisions That Supersede the Earlier Design

1. Staff and visitor views use the same floor geometry and the same room names. The visitor view is no longer a coarse collection of large department rectangles.
2. The standalone public route `/lab-map/[stationCode]`, including `/lab-map/office`, is removed.
3. The visitor map is available only from the active-visit card as an accessible popup dialog.
4. The map-specific personnel mode, assignment UI, and assignment API are removed. Existing database tables and rows are retained for recoverability and are not queried by the map.
5. A new staff sidebar parent item named **ความปลอดภัย** contains **แผนที่ห้องปฏิบัติการ** as its child.

## Source of Truth and Coordinate Model

`Screenshot 2026-07-26 092955.png` is the visual source of truth for the rebuild. The SVG keeps the source plan's aspect ratio and uses one view box for every consumer.

The map model separates five layers that were previously conflated:

1. **Structural geometry:** exterior walls, interior walls, doors, door swings, scanner-control barriers, stairs, and lifts.
2. **Rooms and spaces:** selectable semantic areas, including nested spaces.
3. **Labels:** explicit label anchors, line breaks, font sizes, and optional rotations. Labels are not generated by truncating room names.
4. **Access and safety points:** fingerprint checkpoints, permanently locked doors, exits, and map stations.
5. **Routes:** approved visitor and evacuation polylines drawn over the structural layer.

Structural geometry is rendered once and is never reconstructed from interactive room rectangles. This allows a snack room to sit inside the blood-donation room, a restroom to sit inside the group-head office, and barriers to remain visible without creating fake rooms or covering corridors.

## Geometry Corrections

The rebuild explicitly corrects the reported defects:

- one restroom is nested inside the group-head office;
- lifts 3 and 4 have equal dimensions;
- the donor snack room is nested fully inside the blood-donation room;
- the BSL2 Enhance footprint follows the latest source plan;
- the permanent-lock symbol is anchored to the actual locked door and is not described as the electrical-control room itself;
- scanner-control boundary lines are included at every checkpoint shown in the source plan;
- room names use authored line breaks and anchors so no word is dropped, duplicated, or clipped;
- scanner points, exits 3A/3B/3C, doors, and stations share the same coordinates as the walls and routes.

## Visitor Checkpoint Mapping

Department-to-checkpoint mapping remains explicit and fail-closed. It is not inferred from the nearest room.

The critical correction is:

- **งานอณูชีววิทยา** routes to the fingerprint checkpoint on the scanner boundary beside the PPE zone below Central Lab.
- It must not route to the checkpoint on the boundary of the separate clinical immunology room.

Every visitor route must satisfy all of the following:

- start at the office station used by the active visit;
- remain in public circulation space;
- terminate exactly at the configured fingerprint checkpoint coordinate;
- never continue beyond the checkpoint;
- expose concise, glanceable Thai directions on the active-visit card;
- fail closed with a contact-office message when no approved mapping exists.

Checkpoint codes and their intended departments are tested as a table. Route validation also asserts that the last route coordinate equals the destination checkpoint coordinate.

## Active-Visit Map Popup

The active-visit card keeps its two existing actions, but they become buttons rather than navigation links:

### Open map to checkpoint

- Opens the popup in the area/navigation mode.
- Selects the active visit's exact checkpoint and route.
- Highlights the destination checkpoint and shows that the route ends there.
- Does not change the URL or discard the active visit and checkout controls.

### View exits and safety

- Opens the same popup in safety mode.
- Uses the office station as **คุณอยู่ที่นี่** for this flow.
- Automatically displays the approved primary and alternate evacuation presets for that station.

The popup uses an accessible dialog with a labelled close button, focus management, Escape-to-close, background scroll locking, and focus return to the triggering button. On narrow screens it becomes a near-full-screen sheet. Zoom and pan remain available inside the map without moving the underlying page.

The standalone public map route, its Public Nav entry, and its public-home shortcut are removed. A direct request to the old route returns 404.

## Staff Map and Navigation

The staff sidebar gains this group:

- **ความปลอดภัย**
  - **แผนที่ห้องปฏิบัติการ** → `/staff/lab-map`

The group follows the existing expandable sidebar behavior and opens automatically while the child route is active. The Topbar title remains **แผนที่ห้องปฏิบัติการ**.

The staff map retains only these modes:

- **พื้นที่และหน่วยงาน**
- **เขตควบคุมการติดเชื้อ**
- **ความปลอดภัย**

The map-specific **บุคลากร** mode is removed from the mode type, staff client, search results, details panel, and server DTO. Map assignment forms, map personnel API routes, schemas, server helpers, and tests are removed. The database table is retained but unused; no destructive migration is part of this work.

## Safety Behavior

Safety mode is operationally distinct from the area view:

- shows exits, the permanently locked door, PPE transition context, station marker, and evacuation routes;
- hides fingerprint markers that are unrelated to the active safety route;
- draws the primary route as the dominant solid line and the alternate route as a visually distinct dashed line;
- includes arrowheads that show travel direction;
- labels **คุณอยู่ที่นี่** at the active station;
- includes a legend for primary route, alternate route, exit, and locked door;
- selects presets automatically by station;
- shows no route and instructs the user to follow approved physical signs when a preset is missing.

Evacuation paths are preset data, not shortest-path calculations. The rebuild can align presets to the source drawing, but a release remains a draft until the routes and all access points pass the physical walk-through checklist and are approved by the responsible safety personnel.

## Data Flow

The master manifest owns structural geometry, rooms, labels, access points, stations, and route presets.

- `/staff/lab-map` receives the full staff DTO, including infection classes.
- The active-visit server builds a visitor DTO from the same master geometry and room labels, omitting staff-only metadata and infection classifications.
- `ActiveVisitCard` receives the visitor DTO and opens the popup locally; it does not fetch or navigate to a public map route.
- Print generation uses the same master geometry and approved preset selection.

There is no second hand-maintained public geometry manifest.

## Versioning and Release Safety

The rebuilt geometry receives a new map version and manifest hash. Any release tied to the previous hash no longer qualifies as an official export. Draft printouts keep the existing draft watermark.

The new version may be published only after the floor-3 acceptance checklist verifies:

- every room and nested room against the source plan;
- every fingerprint checkpoint against the physical device and barrier;
- visitor routes by walking from the office to each checkpoint;
- primary and alternate evacuation paths from every supported station;
- exits, the locked door, PPE zone, storage zone, lifts, stairs, and printed readability.

## Testing and Acceptance

Implementation follows test-first development. Automated coverage must include:

- structural geometry invariants for the reported room nesting and equal lift sizes;
- authored labels with no automatic three-line truncation;
- exact department-to-checkpoint mappings, including the molecular/PPE correction;
- visitor route endpoints matching checkpoint coordinates;
- no visitor route crossing a scanner-control boundary or continuing past its checkpoint;
- automatic primary and alternate evacuation selection by station;
- fail-closed behavior for missing evacuation presets;
- route arrowheads, station marker, safety legend, and distinct route variants;
- removal of the personnel mode, map personnel endpoints, and related client code;
- removal of `/lab-map/[stationCode]` and Public Nav/home links;
- active-visit dialog behavior and preservation of self-checkout;
- staff sidebar parent/child behavior;
- public DTO exclusion of personnel and infection-class metadata;
- TypeScript, focused map/visitor/navigation tests, and a production Next.js build.

Visual acceptance must be performed at desktop and mobile sizes and compared side-by-side with the source plan. Automated tests do not replace the physical walk-through required for an official safety release.
